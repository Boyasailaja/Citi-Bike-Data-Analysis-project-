🏍️ Key Insights:

Overall Trip Usage:

The analysis of three months of Citi Bike data helps understand how frequently the bike-sharing service was used during the selected period. The total number of trips gives an overview of the overall demand.

Monthly Usage Pattern

Comparing the three months helps identify which month had higher or lower bike usage. Changes in monthly demand can be observed through the number of trips recorded in each month.

Hourly Usage Pattern

Trip activity varies throughout the day. Examining the number of rides by hour helps identify periods when bike usage was relatively high or low.

Weekday and Weekend Usage

Separating trips into weekdays and weekends helps understand different usage patterns. Weekday trips may reflect regular travel, while weekend trips can represent leisure or recreational travel.

Day-wise Demand

Comparing Monday through Sunday helps identify which days recorded more bike trips. This provides a better understanding of weekly riding patterns.
Rider Type Analysis

The dataset contains different rider categories such as members and casual riders. Comparing these groups helps understand how frequently each type uses the bike-sharing service.

Trip Duration

Average trip duration provides information about how long riders typically use Citi Bikes. Very short and very long trips can also be examined to understand the distribution of ride durations.

Popular Stations

Identifying frequently used start and end stations helps determine locations with higher bike demand. These stations can be useful for understanding areas where bike availability may be particularly important.

Bike Type Usage

If the dataset contains different rideable types, such as electric and classic bikes, their usage can be compared to understand rider preferences.

Weather and Bike Usage

Weather information can be compared with trip activity to observe whether temperature, precipitation, humidity, or wind conditions correspond with changes in bike usage. This shows the relationship between environmental conditions and cycling activity.

Peak Demand Periods

Combining hourly and day-wise analysis helps identify periods with relatively high demand. This can provide useful information for understanding when the bike-sharing system experiences greater usage.

Data Quality

During cleaning, missing values, duplicate rides, invalid timestamps, and unrealistic trip durations were checked. Cleaning these records improves the reliability of the analysis.

Conclusion

The three-month Citi Bike analysis provides an overview of rider behavior, trip patterns, station usage, ride duration, and the relationship between weather and bike usage. The EDA and visualizations make it easier to identify important patterns in Citi Bike demand and provide a foundation for further analysis or machine-learning work.
